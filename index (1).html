About

An economist who builds the models, not just the theory.

I'm finishing a B.Sc. in Economics and Statistics at Chuka University (graduating November 21, 2026, Second Class Upper Division), paired with hands-on professional training: a Data Analytics/Data Science certificate from CDAM (Center of Data Analytics and Modeling), a Generative AI and Microsoft Power BI certificate from Exodus Experts, and a Data Visualization certificate from the Excellerate internship program — where I also led a team building a live analytics dashboard.

My edge is combining an economist's instinct for why a number matters with a data scientist's ability to build the model that predicts it. I work across the full pipeline — cleaning messy data, building and validating machine learning models, and packaging the result into a dashboard or app a non-technical stakeholder can actually use to make a decision.

I've applied this across healthcare risk prediction, fraud detection, customer churn, marketing-mix modeling, and Olympic performance analytics — always with the same goal: turn a dataset into a decision someone can act on today.
